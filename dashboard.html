Learning Content

1. What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt engineering means writing clear instructions for AI tools so they can produce accurate, useful and structured output.

2. Basic Prompt Formula

  • Role: Tell AI who it should act as.
  • Task: Clearly describe what you want.
  • Context: Add background information.
  • Format: Tell AI how the answer should look.

3. Example

Prompt: Act as a resume expert. Improve this BCA student resume summary in a professional tone and keep it under 80 words.
